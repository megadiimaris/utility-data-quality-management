# Utility Operations Data Quality Management – Jos Electricity Distribution Plc

## 📋 Project Overview

This project was executed during my tenure as **Engineer / MIS & Data Quality Officer** at **Ortech Nigeria Ltd**, a consulting firm providing services to **Jos Electricity Distribution Plc**.

I was responsible for maintaining, cleaning, and analyzing operational datasets involving **electricity metering**, **customer information**, and **technical infrastructure**. The goal was to improve data quality, enhance reporting accuracy, and support operational decision-making for Nigeria's electricity distribution network.

## 🔍 Key Activities Performed

### Data Management & Quality Assurance
- Maintained datasets covering electricity metering, customer information, and technical infrastructure
- Performed extensive data cleaning to remove duplicates, correct errors, and standardize formats
- Implemented data entry standards and naming conventions across departments
- Ensured consistent data across multiple databases and reporting systems

### Reporting & Analysis
- Generated management reports on key metrics like customer coverage, meter installation rates, and revenue collection
- Used Excel-based reporting tools to track operational trends and identify areas for improvement
- Created KPI dashboards for management decision-making

### Technical Support
- Supervised AMI/AMR systems for automated meter reading and analysis
- Assisted with low-voltage metering installation, inspection, and troubleshooting
- Managed data center operations and management information systems
